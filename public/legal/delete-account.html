Delete account & data

This page is the external web resource for Google Play account deletion, and mirrors the in-app Delete Account flow required by Apple when accounts exist.

CUSTOMIZE: REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL. After auth ships, keep this page live even if users primarily delete in-app.

In the app (preferred)

  1. Open LoonieWins → Profile (or Settings on mobile).
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. On-device data is cleared immediately. If you are signed in, your account is deleted via our backend.

By email

Email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the address on your account with subject “LoonieWins account deletion”. We will verify and delete associated personal data. Account freezing is not used as a substitute.

What is deleted

  • Account credentials and profile / Smart-Fill data tied to your user id
  • Applied contests, transactions, referral rows, and wins tied to your user id
  • On-device caches and preferences when you use the in-app control

Shared public contest listings are not personal data and remain available to other users. Cancel store subscriptions in Apple ID / Google Play settings if you have an active paid plan.
